How to film for the best results
  • Face-on: camera square to your chest, level with your hands.
  • Down-the-line: camera behind your hands, pointing at the target, about hand height.
  • Use a tripod, and keep your whole body in frame for the entire swing, with the club fully visible at the top.
  • Film in good light against a plain background, in fitted clothing.
  • Slow-motion (120/240 fps) gives the most accurate positions. Trim the clip to one swing if you can.
